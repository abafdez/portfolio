## What we did

- The first thing on `/blog` was a wall of 55 unstyled tag links (in dev; 28 of them on a single post). Looked at how others do it: [Kent C. Dodds](https://kentcdodds.com/blog) (topic pills that filter), [Josh Comeau](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/) (a few categories in a sidebar), [Maggie Appleton](https://maggieappleton.com/garden) (a scrolling row), [Jim Nielsen](https://blog.jim-nielsen.com/tags/) (all tags, on their own page). I picked Josh's two columns.
- `/blog` is two columns from 60rem: posts, and a "Browse by tag" sidebar. On a phone the sidebar is above the posts. The page sets `composition: 'default'` to get out of the blog's narrow reading column.
- `TagCloud.svelte`: chips, most used first, each with its own colour (a hue from a hash of the name, the same `hueOf()` as the generic skill icons, now in `src/lib/hue.ts`): a stripe on the edge, an 8% tint, and the `/about` spotlight ring near the pointer (`spotlight.ts`, moved to `src/lib/`).
- Folded, the list shows a few rows (`--tag-rows`: 9 beside the posts, 4 on a phone) and fades out into a **+** button that shows them all.
- Each chip reads "#css, 26 posts" to a screen reader (it used to be "#css 26").
- The first post card's cover now loads eagerly with `fetchpriority="high"` (a `priority` prop on `TeaserCard`).

## What I learned

- **Fewer, not prettier:** the problem was 55 links, not their style. Sorting by count and folding the long tail fixed more than any styling.
- **Faded isn't hidden:** a tag under the fade can still get keyboard focus, and a screen reader still reads it. So the faded row and everything below it is `inert` until the list opens, measured again by a `ResizeObserver` whenever the list wraps differently.
- **Fold in the CSS, not from `onMount`:** the fold is under `@media (scripting: enabled)` from the start, so the page doesn't jump when the JS arrives; without JS every tag shows and the button is hidden.
- After "show all", focus moves to the first tag that was hidden: what I asked to see.
- A tint is a new background: I checked text on every hue (in steps of 3°), every theme, both modes. The worst is 4.81:1.

## Problems & fixes

- The **+** button showed without JS: my `@media (scripting: none)` rule came before the button's own `display`. Same specificity, so the later rule won. Moved it after.
- Lighthouse performance on `/blog` went from 96 to 91: on a phone the first post's cover is now above the fold and becomes the LCP (3.3 s simulated; the page's first paint is 2.0 s). Eager loading and `fetchpriority` are right for an LCP image but didn't move the simulated number. Accepted for now.
